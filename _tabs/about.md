## Hey, I'm i4m5ilva.

I’m Sedrick Gobina aka I4m5ilva, an offensive security researcher and MSc Network Engineering & Security student based in Cameroon. My goal is simple: chart a direct path to the CISO desk through the front lines of offensive security.

This blog is my digital workbench; a place where I document the systems I break, the mechanics behind the exploits, and the hard-won security insights that only reveal themselves after the third failed attempt.

### What I do

I hold CompTIA Network+, Security+, PenTest+, and CNVP certifications, sharpening my skill set through intensive Hack The Box lab work and CTF competitions. Beyond individual research, I co-lead community efforts with Hack The Box Cameroon and OWASP Cameroon communities; organizing CTF competitions and promoting hands-on cybersecurity education across the region and beyond.

### What you'll find here

- CTF and HackTheBox writeups; techniques, tooling, and the dead ends
  that didn't make it into the official writeup
- Occasional thoughts on the road from security practitioner to security
  leadership
