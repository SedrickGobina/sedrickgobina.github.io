## Hey, I'm i4m5ilva.

I'm Sedrick Gobina — an offensive security researcher and MSc Network Engineering and
Security student based in Cameroon, working toward a CISO-track career
through the offensive security path. This blog is where I write about the
things I break, the things I learn breaking them, and the occasional lesson
that only shows up after the third failed attempt.

### What I do

I hold CompTIA Network+, Security+, PenTest+, and CNVP certifications, and
spend a lot of my time in HackTheBox labs and CTF competitions. I'm active
in both **HackTheBox Cameroon** and **OWASP Cameroon**, where we organize 
CTF competitions and discuss cybersecurity thereby promoting a more cyber-aware culture

Outside of security work, I run **Silva Designs**, a freelance graphic
design practice — which is honestly where a lot of this blog's visual
sensibility comes from.

### What you'll find here

- CTF and HackTheBox writeups — techniques, tooling, and the dead ends
  that didn't make it into the official writeup
- Occasional thoughts on the road from security practitioner to security
  leadership
